Instructor resources

A week-by-week teaching plan for the released chapters. Each week pairs a chapter with the datasets it uses, the Excel skills it builds, and a suggested assignment. Dataset mappings are the author's best fit and may be adapted freely under the CC BY 4.0 license.

WeekChapterTopicsDatasetsExcel skillsSuggested assignment
Week 1What Healthcare Data Analytics Is and Why It MattersTopics: data, information, insight, and action; the four types of analytics; the four domains of healthcare analytics; why healthcare is a distinctive analytics environmentDatasets: DS2_appointments_clean.csv, DS1_facility_dim.csvExcel skills: opening a CSV in Excel; navigating a large fileAssignment: compute the overall no-show rate from the appointment file and write a one-paragraph interpretation of what it does and does not show
